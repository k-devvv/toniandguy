Your appointment details.

When you send an appointment request, we collect your name, mobile number, selected salon and service, preferred date/window, and any optional appointment note. Please avoid including medical documents or other sensitive information.

These details help the salon contact you, arrange your visit and manage your booking. Submitting a request does not confirm an appointment. The salon agrees the final time with you.

Access and storage

Authorised staff can access requests for the branches they manage. Site hosting uses Cloudflare; booking records and staff authentication use Supabase. Contact and request details are not intentionally stored in this website's browser storage. Your selected branch may be remembered on your device. Photos used in the colour preview stay in your browser.

Your choices

Appointment contact consent is separate from marketing. This form does not subscribe you to promotions. Ask the salon to correct or delete your details through its published branch contact, or in person. Staff can refer deletion requests to the account owner.

Security checks use Cloudflare Turnstile, which processes browser/network signals to help prevent spam. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Contact and privacy requests

For booking questions, corrections or deletion requests, call +91 81237 29873 or contact the salon on WhatsApp. Include your booking reference where available. Do not send identity documents through this form.

WhatsApp booking

Choosing a WhatsApp booking link prepares a message containing the details you entered. You decide whether to send it in WhatsApp. The website does not send automatic confirmations or reminders. A saved request is pending until the salon confirms availability.

Retention

Booking records remain in the salon database until the account owner removes them. Automatic expiry is not currently enabled. You can request deletion using the contact details above; records needed for a legal obligation may need to be retained.

Optional website analytics

With your permission, Google Tag Manager loads Google Analytics 4 to measure public page views, saved appointment requests, and clicks on WhatsApp, call and directions controls. A WhatsApp click does not mean a message was sent, and a saved request is not a confirmed appointment. Analytics is off until you choose Allow analytics. Rejecting it does not affect booking.

We send controlled public page paths and titles, event names and a branch code where relevant. We exclude booking names, phone numbers, notes, booking references, appointment dates and selected booking treatments. Query strings and fragments are omitted. Automatic form and outbound-link measurement is disabled so prepared WhatsApp messages are not collected.

Google processes browser/device and network information as part of Analytics. Analytics cookies may recognize returning browsers after consent. Advertising tags, Google signals and advertising personalization are not enabled. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Your analytics choice is stored in this browser for up to 180 days. Use Analytics preferences in the website footer to change it. Withdrawing consent disables future measurement, removes accessible Analytics cookies and reloads the page. Browser Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track preferences keep analytics off. Previously collected data may remain in Google Analytics under its retention settings.
